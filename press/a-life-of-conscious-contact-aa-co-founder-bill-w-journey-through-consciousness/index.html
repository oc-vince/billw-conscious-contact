A Life of Conscious Contact: AA Co-Founder Bill W’s Journey Through Consciousness

September 11, 2026

The Inner Well Podcast

Featuring Jay Stinnett

Every so often, a story comes along that inspires the masses. This is true to the story and life of AA Co-founder Bill Wilson. Having struggled with addiction for a large part of his life, it took one conscious moment to flip the script and rewrite his narrative. Dive into this episode to hear Bill W Conscious Contact producer Jay Stinnett describe the life of Bill W, his evolution through consciousness and what part TM played in the later years of his life.

About the Guest

Jay Stinnett

Jay Stinnett is an independent scholar, biographer, and author. His award-winning book, “Bill W. Conscious Contact: The Award-Winning Film on Paper,” is the culmination of over three decades of writing and presenting on Alcoholics Anonymous (A.A.) history, offering groundbreaking new perspectives on Bill Wilson’s spiritual evolution. An expert on the Oxford Group, Bill Wilson, and Carl Jung, Stinnett has spent years working with the major archives of A.A. history, delving into Dr. Bob and Bill Wilson’s mystical practices, and has conducted pioneering research into Wilson’s role investigating psychedelics.

An engaging keynote speaker, Jay has lectured and led retreats in 18 countries, gaining insights into A.A.’s effectiveness across nations, cultures, and religions. He is an enthusiastic supporter of the Stepping Stones Foundation® — the Historic Home of Bill and Lois Wilson® in Katonah, N.Y. — and was instrumental in creating its digital archive. He is also the author of “Loving Sober: A Field Guide to Spiritual Intimacy” and a longtime member of Initiatives of Change. Jay is a meditation enthusiast living in the “4th Dimension” in Sedona, Arizona.
